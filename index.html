About

Empowering Businesses with Advanced Data Solutions

Hi, I'm Ross Carhart — a data consultant based in Pittsburgh, PA with 10+ years of experience turning complex, messy data into clear business results. I've built customer data models for a major national retailer, grown a subscription business 200% through better measurement, and automated reporting that saved analysts thousands of hours.

My core toolkit is modern data warehousing (Snowflake, Databricks), analytics engineering (dbt, Python, SQL), and BI (Power BI, Tableau). Increasingly, I help businesses put AI to work — from LLM-assisted marketing optimization to fully automated data workflows that run themselves.

I take on consulting engagements of all sizes: one-off dashboard builds, data warehouse design, ongoing fractional analytics support, or AI automation projects. If your data feels like a bottleneck instead of an accelerator, let's talk.
